EarLab – foreground service demo

This video shows the only foreground service in EarLab, the optional connection monitor (foreground service type: connected device). The user starts it on the Connection screen with "Monitor for 10 minutes". A persistent notification shows the remaining time and states that no audio is recorded. The service keeps running while the app is in the background, can be stopped from the app or from the notification, and ends by itself when the chosen time runs out.
